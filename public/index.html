little faqs

the cute + useful stuff
i've never done yoga or pilates. is that okay?

babe, that's perfect. every class is for every level, and teachers show you an easier and a harder version of each move. no one is watching you, promise.

i'm new. where do i start?

new members get 7 days of unlimited classes for $30. try a few and find your favorite. Beginner Yoga on Sundays at 10:30 am is a sweet first class.

what should i bring?

comfy clothes you can move in, a water bottle, and yourself. if you have your own mat, bring it along.

do you have prenatal classes?

yes, mama. Prenatal is Monday and Wednesday at 6 pm, and Perinatal is Saturday at 10 am. check in with your doctor or midwife first.
